Add cinematographer as a fourth entity kind, and 10 DPs to start

WatchOrder now covers directors, actors, studios and cinematographers.
A cinematographer's pair schema mirrors a director's (signature, gateway,
must_see, note) with no new validation branch needed — the "not actor,
not studio" path in validate.mjs already fit. New work: a `cinematographer`
kind in src/core/kinds.js (chrono-meaningful, diversityDelta 1.35 — tuned
up from an initial 1.0 after several thin 7-film entities converged on
near-identical 3-film selections regardless of profile), a fourth
KIND_SECTIONS entry and KIND_LABEL/copy updates in pages.js and search.js,
and a custom mode-question label ("Watch the eye develop").

Ten cinematographers, chosen to lean hard on the "tag a film once"
architecture: Roger Deakins, Emmanuel Lubezki, Robert Elswit, Conrad Hall,
Robert Richardson, Janusz Kamiński, Christopher Doyle, Sven Nykvist,
Hoyte van Hoytema, Gordon Willis. Most of their filmographies were already
tagged for the directors and actors they shot for — only 4 new films
needed tagging (1917, Manhattan, In Cold Blood, Let the Right One In).
Each pair's `note` describes the cinematography specifically (lighting,
camera movement, color, format) rather than restating the film's plot,
the same differentiation the actor schema uses for showcase vs signature.

0 validate errors, 29/30 tests pass (only the pre-existing register-axis
gap remains, proportionally unchanged).
